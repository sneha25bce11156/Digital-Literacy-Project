# Project Overview

This repository contains my Digital Literacy Project submitted as part of the CSE0001 course. The project helped me understand the importance of digital literacy in academic, personal, and professional life. Through this project, I explored different aspects of digital awareness such as creating a digital literacy infographic, building a student digital portfolio, using coding and collaboration platforms, practicing professional email etiquette, and learning about cybercrime awareness.

The work is organized into five tasks, each representing one module of the course. All screenshots, documents, and write-ups are arranged in separate folders for clarity and easy access.

# Repository Structure

task-1-presentation/ – Digital Literacy infographic or poster
task-2-portfolio/ – Screenshots of professional and academic profiles
task-3-platforms/ – Coding platform activity and Google Form screenshots
task-4-email-etiquette/ – Professional email drafts and social media checklist
task-5-cybercrime/ – Cybercrime case study and prevention checklist
report/ – Final project report in PDF or DOCX format

# Module Summaries

## Task 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic

In this task, I created an infographic on digital literacy using Canva. The infographic explains the meaning of digital literacy and highlights its importance for students, including safe internet use, responsible online behavior, and maintaining a professional online presence.

## Task 2 – Student Digital Portfolio

In this task, I created and updated my profiles on platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and Kaggle. This helped me understand the importance of building a professional digital identity from the beginning of my academic journey.

## Task 3 – Coding and Collaboration Platforms

In this task, I explored a coding platform and solved a beginner-level problem on HackerRank. I also created a Google Form quiz on digital literacy and linked it to a Google Sheet to view responses, which helped me understand digital collaboration tools.

## Task 4 – Professional Email and Etiquette Guide

In this task, I wrote two professional emails: one requesting an assignment extension and another applying for an internship. I also created a checklist of social media do’s and don’ts to promote responsible online behavior.

## Task 5 – Cybercrime Awareness Case Study and Prevention Guide

In this task, I studied a cybercrime case related to phishing and online fraud and created a prevention checklist. This helped me understand common cyber risks and the importance of protecting personal information.
